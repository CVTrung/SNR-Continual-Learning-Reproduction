# Critical analysis

Status: investigation prompts, not established findings.

The course requires at least three strengths, two theoretical limitations, and two future directions tied to the limitations. For each claim record assumptions/theorems, evidence, practical consequences, and a falsifiable experiment.

Questions to investigate include activity-estimation assumptions under dependent/bursty inputs, the gap between oracle resets and practical estimates, and architectural dependence. These are hypotheses, not proven weaknesses. Distinguish correlation from causation and training performance from generalization.
